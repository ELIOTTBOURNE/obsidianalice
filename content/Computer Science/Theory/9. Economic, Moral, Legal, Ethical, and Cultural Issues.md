### Professional Behaviour

- **Purpose**: Defines acceptable use of technology (e.g., no hacking, respecting copyright).  

- **Examples**:  

  - Schools: Banning social media during lessons.  

  - Companies: Prohibiting unauthorized data sharing.  

  

**Violation Consequences**: Detention (schools), termination (workplaces).
